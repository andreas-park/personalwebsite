## The Paper

**Title:** [A 2022 Primer on Crypto Trading](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4148717)
**Author:** Andreas Park
**Prepared for:** Global Risk Institute, June 2022

## Key Question

How does cryptocurrency trading actually work, and how does it compare to traditional financial markets?

## Main Findings

The crypto trading ecosystem has evolved rapidly and now encompasses a wide range of trading venues and mechanisms:

**Centralized Exchanges (CEXs)** like Binance, Coinbase, and Kraken operate similarly to traditional stock exchanges, using order books where buyers and sellers submit limit and market orders. However, they differ in important ways — most notably in their custody arrangements and the lack of regulatory oversight comparable to traditional securities exchanges.

**Decentralized Exchanges (DEXs)** like Uniswap and Curve represent a fundamentally new approach to trading. Instead of matching buyers with sellers through an order book, they use liquidity pools and automated market makers (AMMs) where prices are determined algorithmically based on the ratio of assets in the pool.

**Unique Features of Crypto Markets** include 24/7 trading, permissionless access, self-custody of assets, and the composability of DeFi protocols. These features create both opportunities and risks that don't exist in traditional markets.

## Why It Matters

Understanding the infrastructure of crypto trading is essential for regulators, investors, and researchers alike. The primer was written for the Global Risk Institute to help financial sector professionals understand the plumbing of crypto markets — knowledge that is increasingly important as traditional finance and DeFi converge.

## My Take

One of the most fascinating aspects of crypto trading is how it has replicated — and in some cases reinvented — centuries of financial market evolution in just a few years. AMMs, for instance, provide liquidity without the need for professional market makers, but they introduce new challenges like impermanent loss and MEV extraction. As someone who studies market microstructure, watching this real-time experiment in market design is extraordinary.
