Welcome to my new blog! I'll be posting here roughly every other week with summaries and commentary on interesting research papers in finance, FinTech, market microstructure, and decentralized finance.

Each post will typically cover:

- **The paper's key question** — What problem is the paper trying to solve?
- **Main findings** — What did the authors discover?
- **Why it matters** — How does this connect to broader trends in financial markets?
- **My take** — Personal commentary and connections to other work

If you'd like to discuss a post, feel free to leave a comment below using your GitHub account. I moderate all comments to keep the discussion productive.

Stay tuned for the first paper summary!
