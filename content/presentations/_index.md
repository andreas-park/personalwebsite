## Presentation Setup

### Recording & Streaming Tools
- **Software:** OBS Studio and Streamflow

### Seated Presentations
- Canon EOS RP camera
- Shure SMB7 microphone with Focusrite amplifier
- Godox ML60 lighting with diffuser
- Green screen curtain
- PowerPoint or slides.com for slides

### Standing Presentations
- Rode or Logitech Streamcam
- Amazon lighting kit
- Green wall backdrop
- slides.com for slides
