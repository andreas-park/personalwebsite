Andreas Park is a Professor of Finance at the University of Toronto and serves as the Academic Director at the FinHub, Rotman's Financial Innovation Lab. His research focuses on the economic impact of technological transformations in financial markets, with current work on tokenization, automated market makers applied to equity trading and foreign exchange, and blockchain-based financial infrastructure.

He has previously held the limited-term Canadian Securities Institute's Research Foundation Chair, headed the CSA Data Fee Methodology and Retail Access to Real Time Data Committees, and served on the Ontario Securities Commission's Market Structure Advisory Committee. He serves as an associate editor at the Journal of Finance and Management Science. He has advised regulatory agencies, FinTech start-ups, and large banks, and served as a lab economist at the Creative Destruction Lab.

Andreas co-organizes the microstructure.exchange and the Crypto and Blockchain Economics Research forum (CBER) webinar series and annual conference, and co-hosts the Crafting the Crypto-Economy podcast. He teaches undergraduate, graduate, and executive courses on payments innovation, blockchain, decentralized finance, and financial market trading.
