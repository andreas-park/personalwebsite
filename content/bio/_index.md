I am a Professor of Finance at the University of Toronto, where I have been since 2003. I am based in the Department of Management at UTM, with cross-appointments to the Rotman School of Management and the Institute of Management and Innovation. I held the **Canadian Securities Institute Research Foundation Limited-Term Professorship** from 2022 to 2025 and served as Associate Chair of the Management Department from 2016 to 2019.

I am the Academic Director and a co-founder of [FinHub](https://www.rotman.utoronto.ca/finhub), Rotman's financial innovation research hub, and I co-founded [LedgerHub](https://ledgerhub.utoronto.ca), the university's blockchain research laboratory. I previously served as an economist for the Blockchain stream at the [Creative Destruction Lab](https://www.creativedestructionlab.com/) and have provided consulting services to the Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) and the Investment Industry Regulatory Organization of Canada (IIROC).

I serve as an Associate Editor of the **Journal of Finance** (since 2026) and **Management Science** (since 2022). I co-founded The Microstructure Exchange and the Crypto and Blockchain Economics Research Forum (CBER).

My research focuses on **financial market structure** and **FinTech**. I study how technological innovations—including blockchain, decentralized finance, and algorithmic trading—reshape financial markets, using both theoretical and empirical methods. I have published in journals including *Econometrica*, the *Journal of Finance*, the *Journal of Financial Economics*, and *Management Science*.

From 2023 to 2025, I co-hosted the **[Crafting the Cryptoeconomy](/podcast/)** podcast with Fahad Saleh, exploring the frontiers of blockchain research and regulation.

I hold a PhD in Economics from the **University of Cambridge** and a Diplom (M.Sc.) in mathematical economics from **Bielefeld University**.

Previously, I was co-director of UofT's Master in Financial Economics program and spent 2014–2015 as a visiting professor at Copenhagen Business School.
